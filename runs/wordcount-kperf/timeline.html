Each fork from JVM start to exit, on the wall clock.
?
Startup is JVM boot and benchmark setup; exit runs from the last iteration to the JVM's exit (with xctrace, it includes saving the trace). GC pauses come from the fork's GC log. With async-profiler, VM operations at a safepoint and JIT compilations (C1, C2) and deoptimizations come from the JDK's events in its recording, which covers the last fork's measurement iterations; bars closer than a pixel merge. With xctrace, counter lanes cover the measurement iterations, which is what xctrace records; cycles are per second, so one busy performance core is about 4–4.5 G. Each counter lane has its own scale. Wheel or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to reset.
